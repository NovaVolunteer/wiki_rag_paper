59 DS-3021 course questions, answered by claude-opus-5 over the full wiki, graded 1–10 by claude-haiku-4-5. Intervals are 95% BCa bootstrap, 20,000 resamples of the question set. Resampling captures question-sampling error only — not judge or answerer stochasticity, which need repeated runs.
