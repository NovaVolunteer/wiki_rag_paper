# Decision: ATA and DSLabs to partner on DARPA FALCON SBIR

**Date:** 2026-07-20
**Owners:** [[../people/jonathan-seale]], [[../people/brian-wright]]

## Decision

ATA and DSLabs will partner to submit a proposal to the DARPA FALCON SBIR (Opportunity ID: DPA26BZ04-DV016, DoW SBIR 2026 BAA Release 4). The solicitation opens July 22, 2026 and closes August 19, 2026 at 12:00 PM ET. This is a Direct to Phase II (DP2) solicitation — no Phase I award; the proposal must demonstrate prior research experience in its place.

## What FALCON is

**Full name:** Fusion of Abstract Learning and Context-Optimized Neural-methods

**Objective:** Combine advanced ML methods (computationally efficient on structured/tabular data) with LLMs (general-purpose, contextual) to produce powerful, efficient technology for interactive statistical analysis of large-scale structured data — enterprise and battlefield applications.

**What the work requires:**
- Survey and research emerging ML methods suitable for large-scale structured + unstructured data.
- Develop an architecture combining select ML methods with one or more LLMs (preferably open source).
- Define metrics covering accuracy, new insights, computational efficiency, and cross-dataset generalization.
- Evaluate the combined architecture on datasets from multiple application areas.
- Develop and demonstrate hallucination-mitigation methods with verifiable, reproducible analytic traces.
- Demonstrate interactive analysis — incorporating new insights as they develop during analysis.

**Phase II structure (if awarded):**
- Base year (12 months): initial demo at Month 6; enterprise-scale demo on 2+ datasets at Month 11; final report + software delivery at Month 12.
- Option (6 months): continued technical and commercialization development through Month 18.

**Critical technology areas (OUSD R&E):** Advanced Computing and Software; Trusted AI and Autonomy.

**Keywords:** Neuro-symbolic AI, large-scale structured data analysis, decision theory.

**Reference:** https://www.darpa.mil/research/programs/falcon

## Rationale

FALCON's core problem — combining ML's statistical power on structured data with LLM contextual reasoning — maps directly onto work ATA and DSLabs are already doing: structured medical/operational data, LLM-assisted extraction, and the ATA knowledge assistant's need to reason over linked structured facts. The DP2 format rewards documented prior research, which the team can draw on.

Jonathan has been tracking DARPA and NSF funding opportunities for the knowledge-assistant project (see [[../people/jonathan-seale]]); FALCON is a concrete vehicle to pursue that interest with DSLabs as the technical partner.
